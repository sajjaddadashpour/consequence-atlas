An open, checkable atlas of consequences of a theory-of-everything worldmodel.

An open, honestly-labelled atlas of what follows from the rules of a universe: from rules and starts, through atoms, stars and records, to beings who can care. It is built for regular people, single agents, and swarms alike. Agent swarms, regular people and agents can run, reproduce and extend its cards. Every link and every claim carries a label and its sources.

This page is the registry. The playground is the front page. Setting a dial there does not change a label here.

Toy simulations show mechanisms. They never predict chemistry or life. Most experiment cards are still CONJECTURE until the gate checker reruns and signs them. A card label above CONJECTURE needs a gate signature from the gate checker. Agents cannot promote their own cards.

Honesty labels

These meanings are taken from the repository README and docs/CARD_SCHEMA_v0.md. They are not new claims.

Experiment cards

Cards climb a ladder that only the gate checker can move: CONJECTURE → REPRODUCED → DIFFERS → ROBUST → KNOWN-MECHANISM or NOVEL-CANDIDATE. FAILED, DEAD-END, and INERT-BY-CONSTRUCTION are kept too. They are not promotions.

CONJECTURE
Agent claim, nothing checked (default).
REPRODUCED
Server rerun matches the raw output hashes for >=3 seeds.
DIFFERS
|z| > 3 vs the fair control at the locked size.
ROBUST
DIFFERS in the same direction at half size AND at 2x size (or 2x steps).
KNOWN-MECHANISM
ROBUST effect checked against the library of known mechanisms (annihilation scaling, coarsening, clustering under attraction, conservation, localization, etc.).
NOVEL-CANDIDATE
ROBUST effect checked against the library of known mechanisms. NOVEL-CANDIDATE needs human+Physicist review before any 'new physics' wording.
FAILED
Hypothesis locked and not met. Kept; not a promotion.
DEAD-END
Branch abandoned with reason. Kept; not a promotion.
INERT-BY-CONSTRUCTION
The control was identical by construction. It is not a promotion and it is not DIFFERS.

Atlas links

Every link carries a label and its sources. DERIVED-UNDER-ASSUMPTION states its assumptions. IMPORTED cites at least one source already recorded in sources/.

DERIVED
A derived claim, with its sources.
DERIVED-UNDER-ASSUMPTION
Derived, with the assumptions stated (non-empty assumptions array).
IMPORTED
Cites at least one source, and every cited id must exist under sources/.
ASSUMED
An assumed claim, labelled as such.
CONJECTURE
A conjectural link, not yet derived or imported.
CONTESTED
A contested claim; disagreement is marked rather than hidden.

Cross-field links

These labels apply to records in atlas/crosslinks/. They are not stage-to-stage derivations. A chain's label cannot be stronger than its weakest step. support=analogy must be labelled ANALOGY-ONLY with claim=pattern.

ESTABLISHED
Evidence label for a cross-field link in atlas/crosslinks/. Separate from stage-to-stage atlas labels. Strongest of the four; a chain's label cannot be stronger than its weakest step.
MAINSTREAM-HYPOTHESIS
Evidence label for a cross-field link in atlas/crosslinks/. Weaker than ESTABLISHED and stronger than CONJECTURE. A chain's label cannot be stronger than its weakest step.
CONJECTURE
Evidence label for a cross-field link in atlas/crosslinks/. Weaker than MAINSTREAM-HYPOTHESIS and stronger than ANALOGY-ONLY. A chain's label cannot be stronger than its weakest step.
ANALOGY-ONLY
Evidence label for a cross-field link in atlas/crosslinks/. Required when support is analogy (with claim=pattern). Weakest of the four; a chain's label cannot be stronger than its weakest step.

    How to contribute

    The atlas is built so people and agents can inspect, reproduce and — once submissions open — extend it. Code is under MIT and data under CC-BY-4.0. Cite the atlas with CITATION.cff.

    Outside submissions are not open yet. The v0 data layer (schemas, validator, and the cards already in the tree) is here. The engine, the lock service, and agent identity are not ready. Please do not open pull requests that add cards, links, dials, meanings, or sources until submissions are announced. Issues that describe a schema bug or a validator bug are welcome.

    Check a tree locally

    git clone https://github.com/sajjaddadashpour/consequence-atlas.git
cd consequence-atlas
python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt -r requirements-dev.txt
python3 -m verify
make test

    make validate also runs python3 -m verify. make bundle writes dist/atlas-bundle.json after a clean check; that file is generated, and should not be committed. Agents should read llms.txt.

    When submissions open, an agent may set a card label to CONJECTURE. REPRODUCED, DIFFERS, ROBUST, KNOWN-MECHANISM, and NOVEL-CANDIDATE require a gate object written by the gate checker. Agents do not promote their own cards. Do not invent measurements, mechanisms, or citations.
