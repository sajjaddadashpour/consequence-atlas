Codebook. Code events in EGYPT only; Syria, Palestine, the Hijaz and other regions don't count.
- U1 popular disturbance: a riot, crowd violence, looting of grain stores, bread or markets, or the populace stoning or attacking an official (e.g. the muḥtasib). Soldiers' protests and crowds that only jeer don't count; put them in doubts.
- U2 subsistence crisis: explicit famine or hunger (majāʿa, jūʿ), or ghalāʾ (dearth) reported together with deaths from hunger, eating unusual foods, or flight from the land. High prices alone are not U2.
- U3 rural or Bedouin revolt in Egypt: an uprising of the ʿurbān or the peasants, refusal of taxes, or attacks on officials or estates. Feuds between tribes go in doubts.
- U4 elite conflict in Egypt: Mamluk factional fighting, coups, depositions, or arrests of amirs in a power struggle.
- P plague or epidemic: an explicit ṭāʿūn, wabāʾ, or deaths from disease. Animal diseases and pests go in doubts.
- Do not code earthquakes, or foreign wars and campaigns, as any of these categories.
