## Claim (mechanism chain; physics -> climate -> hydrology -> society)
Large sulfate-rich eruptions cool the NH and weaken the African/Ethiopian summer monsoon, the Nile flood fails, the next harvest fails, and grain prices, famine and unrest follow.
- Dial type: **TARGET** (who bears a shared shock: whose outcome counts).
- **evidence_rung 2** for L1 and L3: eruption timing is exogenous to Egyptian society, so it works as a natural experiment (`rung_basis`: exogenous ice-core-dated eruption timing). **L2 is rung 1**: recorded Nilometer maxima are not fully exogenous (officials announced the level; manipulation of announcements is discussed by Popper 1951), so flood-to-unrest is observational.
- **model_exists false.** No quantitative model of the chain is fitted or predicted from.

## Series (fixed now)
Window: CE years 1250-1517 inclusive, L = 268 years (Mamluk sultanate 1250 to the Ottoman conquest 1517).

### E: eruptions
- Source: **eVolv2k version 2**, Toohey & Sigl 2017, *ESSD* 9(2):809-831, doi:10.5194/essd-9-809-2017; dataset doi:10.1594/WDCC/eVolv2k_v2 (DataCite record checked). Columns used: year, VSSI (Tg S), assigned source latitude/hemisphere class.
- Selection: every event with **VSSI >= 5.0 Tg S** whose eVolv2k source assignment is tropical or NH extratropical. Events assigned to the SH extratropics are excluded (they cannot plausibly reach the NH monsoon; Oman 2006 mechanism). Threshold chosen before the list was seen; it is roughly half of the Tambora value (28.1 Tg S per eVolv2k, as used in item 3).
- Key year e = eVolv2k event year (ice-core deposition-start year; Manning 2017 uses the same convention). Events within 3 years of an earlier selected event are merged into it (key year = the first), so overlapping windows are not double-counted.
- Dating uncertainty: ice-core dating error of about ±1 yr is absorbed by the 0-2 yr window; the exact Sigl et al. 2015 uncertainty figure for this period is NOT VERIFIED. Unscored variant V3 widens the window to -1..+3.
- An event in 1248-1249 with window reaching into 1250 is included (its post-window years that fall inside 1250-1517 are counted).

### F: Nile flood maxima
- Source: the Roda Nilometer annual maxima as compiled by **Kondrashov, Feliks & Ghil 2005**, *GRL* 32(10), 2004GL022156, doi:10.1029/2004GL022156 (the series Manning 2017 used), built on Toussoun (1925, *Mémoire sur l'histoire du Nil*) and Popper (1951, *The Cairo Nilometer: Studies in Ibn Taghrī Birdī's Chronicles of Egypt I*, University of California Publications in Semitic Philology 12; reviews verified: *Speculum* 27:417-419, doi:10.2307/2853119). Coverage after about 1468 (the end of Ibn al-Hijazi's list per Chaney's supplement) is NOT VERIFIED.
- Gaps: only raw recorded maxima are used. Any value that the dataset documentation marks as gap-filled or interpolated (e.g. SSA-filled) is set to missing. Missing years stay missing; nothing is imputed. If the documentation does not allow raw and filled values to be told apart, L1 and L2 are NOT-TESTABLE-YET (L3 does not need F).
- Low-flood year: raw maximum in the **bottom 20%** of the non-missing 1250-1517 maxima.

### U: unrest / subsistence crisis (no coded series exists; coded now by protocol)
- Texts: al-Maqrīzī, *al-Sulūk li-maʿrifat duwal al-mulūk*, for Hijri years 648-844 (OpenITI `0845Maqrizi.Suluk`, repo OpenITI/0850AH; existence checked via repository metadata only, text not opened); Ibn Iyās, *Badāʾiʿ al-zuhūr fī waqāʾiʿ al-duhūr*, for 845-923 (OpenITI `0930IbnIyas.BadaicZuhur`, repo OpenITI/0950AH; same). Version: latest OpenITI release on download day, recorded with its file hash.
- Event types counted (Egypt only; Syria, Hijaz and elsewhere not counted):
  - U1 popular disturbance: riot, crowd violence, looting of grain stores, bread or markets, stoning or attack on an official (e.g. the *muḥtasib*) by the populace.
  - U2 subsistence crisis: explicit famine or hunger (*majāʿa*, *jūʿ*), or *ghalāʾ* (dearth) reported together with deaths from hunger, eating of unusual foods, or flight from the land.
  - U3 rural or Bedouin revolt in Egypt: uprising of the *ʿurbān* or peasants, refusal of taxes, attacks on officials or estates.
  - Not counted: plague or epidemic alone; earthquakes; foreign wars. Mamluk factional fighting, coups and depositions are coded as U4 and are **unscored**.
- Annual outcome U_y = 1 if at least one U1-U3 event in year y, else 0. Year assignment: Hijri month and year as stated, mapped to CE by a fixed script; if only the Hijri year is given, the event goes to the CE year containing that Hijri year's midpoint.

## Tests (one statistic per link, fixed now)
Lag window W(t) = {t, t+1, t+2} (0-2 years after the shock year t), clipped to 1250-1517. Missing F years are excluded from the F-based means.
- **L1 eruption -> low flood:** D1 = mean F over years in ∪_e {e, e+1} minus mean F over all other non-missing years. Predicted D1 < 0.
- **L2 low flood -> unrest:** D2 = mean U over years in ∪_{low t} W(t) minus mean U over all other years. Predicted D2 > 0.
- **L3 eruption -> unrest (the new link):** D3 = mean U over years in ∪_e W(e) minus mean U over all other years. Predicted D3 > 0.

**Primary null: circular shift.** For L1 and L3, rotate the set of eruption key years by a random offset k (years mod 268 within 1250-1517); for L2, rotate the whole flood series (missing years travel with it). U is never shifted. This keeps each series' own clustering, so chroniclers' bunching of bad news cannot by itself produce an effect. Offsets are drawn uniformly from k ∈ {3, ..., 265} (excluding shifts so small that windows overlap the observed ones), **10,000 draws**, numpy default_rng seed 20261119. One-sided p = (1 + #{D_null as or more extreme in the predicted direction than observed}) / (1 + 10,000). Because only 263 distinct rotations exist, the exact enumeration over all 263 offsets is also reported; the 10,000-draw p is the scored one, and if the two fall on opposite sides of 0.05 the exact enumeration governs. Resolution limit ≈ 1/264 ≈ 0.004, below the threshold.
- Threshold: **p < 0.05**, one-sided, per link. No correction across L1-L3 because L1 and L2 are replication checks and only L3 is the new claim.
- Unscored comparison: an iid shuffle of years (10,000 draws, same seed). It breaks clustering and is expected to overstate significance; reported only to show the size of that effect.

## Verdicts (no MIXED)
Minimum data: at least 6 selected eruptions with key year in 1244-1517 (window touching 1250-1517); for L1, at least 6 of them with a non-missing F in {e, e+1}; for U, at least 80% of Hijri years segmentable and coder agreement Cohen's κ >= 0.6 on U_y. Failing any of these makes the affected link NOT-TESTABLE-YET, naming the missing piece.
- **L1:** D1 < 0 and p < 0.05 -> **SURVIVED, counted as REDUCES-TO-KNOWN** (Manning 2017). D1 >= 0 -> **KILLED** (for the Mamluk subset only; it does not overturn Manning's pooled result). Otherwise NOT-TESTABLE-YET.
- **L2:** D2 > 0 and p < 0.05 -> **SURVIVED, counted as REDUCES-TO-KNOWN** (Chaney 2013, to 1437). D2 <= 0 -> **KILLED**. Otherwise NOT-TESTABLE-YET.
- **L3:** D3 > 0 and p < 0.05 -> **SURVIVED** (new; rung 2). D3 <= 0 -> **KILLED**. D3 > 0 with p >= 0.05 -> NOT-TESTABLE-YET.
- Chain card: SURVIVED only if L3 SURVIVED; KILLED if L3 KILLED; else NOT-TESTABLE-YET. L1 and L2 verdicts are reported beside it and cannot upgrade it.

(a) The sentence-redaction list adds the following, applied mechanically after tashkeel is stripped.

Usual prefixes, at most one, longest first: وال، فال، بال، كال، لل، ال، و، ف، ب، ك، ل.

A token matches stem S if the token itself, or the token after removing at most one usual prefix (longest first), is S or S plus one usual ending. The unprefixed form is tried first, so a stem that begins with و، ف، ب، ك or ل (فيضان, بحر, بحار) is not stripped.

- فيضان (all forms): a sentence is blanked if any token matches stem فيضان.
- خليج (all forms): a sentence is blanked if any token matches stem خليج or stem خلجان (the broken plural).
- غريق / غرق: a sentence is blanked only if it has both (i) a token matching one of the stems غرق، غريق، غرقى، أغرق، اغرق، إغراق، اغراق، يغرق، تغرق، نغرق، مغرق, and (ii) a token matching one of the water, river or ship stems ماء، مياه، نهر، أنهار، انهار، انهر، بحر، بحار، سفينة، سفينه، سفن، مركب، مراكب.
