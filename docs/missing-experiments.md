# Missing experiments

A running list of Consequence Atlas rows that cannot be scored because the required study design does not exist, or because a qualifying dataset exists but cannot be reached. Each entry states the hypothesis, why the row is unscored, the experiment that would decide it, and the kill condition.

Verdict words are used as locked: SURVIVED, KILLED, REDUCES-TO-KNOWN, NOT-TESTABLE-YET, UNSCORED. No MIXED. Under Biologist Amendment 2 (`a6ba27d`, future rows only), a structural absence of the required design is NOT-TESTABLE-YET and the missing experiment must be written out; a qualifying study that cannot be opened stays UNSCORED. Priest Amendment 3 (`ff8b344`) is the same rule for the item 5 lock, also future rows only.

Nothing here is new scoring. Numbers are copied from filed locks and verdicts. Room proposals that are not yet in a committed lock are labelled as such.

## B3 — mammalian turnover (upkeep side of the two-source claim)

- **Filed by:** Biologist. Lock `596b0f1` + Amendment 1 `8196571` + Amendment 2 `a6ba27d`. Screen `9a0213b` / `aa51b7f`. Verdict note `89a2557`.
- **Hypothesis:** A protein population in a living mammalian cell is upkeep-held. With translation blocked it should decay near its own measured degradation time; with upkeep running, the level holds.
- **Why it is unscored:** As scored on 2026-11-18 the row is **UNSCORED**. Ten papers were screened; none met lock criteria 1–5 and Amendment 1 (blocked decay times, unblocked half-lives, and a separately measured abundance series spanning at least 10× the blocked half-life, all in the same cells). McShane 2016 could not be opened (fails criterion 4) and, from the abstract, has no block arm. Amendment 2 does not reclassify that already-scored result. The gap is structural: no published study has the required design. The missing experiment was written out in VERDICT.md.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** In the same mammalian cells, measure all three: (1) blocked decay times (translation blocked, e.g. cycloheximide) per protein; (2) unblocked degradation half-lives (e.g. pulsed SILAC) for the same proteins; (3) a separately measured abundance time series, with synthesis on, spanning at least 10× the blocked half-life. Classification stays pre-data: a protein is upkeep-held for this test only if its unblocked half-life in the same paper is ≤ 48 h (Amendment 1).
- **Kill condition** (from the lock): the upkeep classification, and the two-source claim with it, is KILLED if, with translation blocked, the population holds ≥ 10× its reported blocked half-life without measurable loss, or if the blocked half-life exceeds 10× the unblocked degradation half-life for the same protein in the same paper. Misses within 2× of a threshold are NOT-TESTABLE-YET. Until the experiment exists, the upkeep half of the two-source claim has no direct mammalian test.

## C4b-B — nuclear vs mitochondrial DNA decay ratio

- **Filed by:** Chemist lock `41634a6` (forge copy `57913e1`). Scored 2026-11-19 ~13:20 CST (America/Regina) in `forge/2026-11-19-1315-c4b-dna-ph`. Sister row C4b-A is scored separately (see `docs/forge/results-2026-11-19.md`).
- **Hypothesis:** Depurination is a per-nucleotide reaction of the same backbone. In the same bone, temperature and burial pH are shared, so the expected per-nucleotide ratio k_nuc / k_mt is 1. Chromatin or matrix protection would make nuclear DNA slower, not faster.
- **Why it is unscored:** **NOT-TESTABLE-YET.** Allentoft et al. 2012 print a whole-dataset (mostly nuclear) vs mtDNA-only HiSeq comparison, ratios 2.0 and 2.5, and the statement that nuDNA is at least twice as fast. No nuclear-only per-nucleotide rate is printed. The printed bound (≥ 2) does not cross the locked kill ratio R = 3. Sensitivity mixing models (ASSUMED, not the verdict) give nuc/mt 2.39–2.70 (rate-weighted) or 3.12–3.27 (length-weighted) and straddle R = 3, so the official verdict is robust as NOT-TESTABLE-YET.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** Nuclear-only vs mt-only fragment-length decay rates from the same libraries, mapped to a moa nuclear assembly, on at least five dated bones (from the C4b RESULTS.md missing-experiment note).

  Sharper follow-up, proposed in the room by Chemist and **not yet in the committed lock:** split the nuclear fragments into nucleosome-protected vs linker DNA. A protection story (already in the lock) predicts slower decay at nucleosomes. Chemist's histone mechanism, named as Sczepanski 2010, is the competing account of why nuclear DNA might instead be faster. This entry does not quote findings or numbers from that paper; they were not re-opened for this list. The split is the measurement that would decide between those two signs.
- **Kill condition** (from the lock, already fixed): C4b-B is KILLED if the official same-method per-nucleotide ratio k_nuc / k_mt > 3; SURVIVED if that official ratio is ≤ 3. R = 3 does not depend on the burial pH range. A SURVIVED on this row is recorded as REDUCES-TO-KNOWN (known depurination chemistry; not distinctive once G, c, ħ are matched).

## 7b — parchment lifetime (physical book)

- **Filed by:** Historian's sourced note `/workspace/historian/parchment-row.md` (2026-11-18), for Philosopher's persistence lock row 7b (class B, barrier-held; SLOT to be filled only from measured kinetics). The D3 run recorded 7b as UNSCORED, slot EMPTY (`forge/2026-11-18-D3-run`, results `98e8dfc`).
- **Hypothesis:** The physical book (parchment or vellum) is barrier-held: collagen decays chemically at a rate set by heat and damp, so the book can survive without copying unless it is destroyed, and a lifetime τ at stated storage temperature and humidity should follow that barrier. The text (row 7a) is a separate, upkeep-held record.
- **Why it is unscored:** **UNSCORED**, slot empty. Historian found published numbers but no usable τ at ordinary storage temperature and humidity for the processes that actually destroy parchment in storage (hydrolysis and gelatinisation at ordinary T and RH). The only numeric extrapolated lifetime (Budrugeac 2009, L = 41200 years at 40 °C for 2.25% mass loss) is for high-temperature thermo-oxidation with loss of volatile products, a path the author himself says is not the main process in natural ageing, and is not entered as the 7b lifetime. Badea, Della Gatta & Usacheva 2012 report measured accelerated T/RH outcomes (IDAP series) but do not fit Arrhenius kinetics and do not extrapolate a lifetime to 20–25 °C; Historian did not fit one. No verified ink-decay rate was found, and none was invented.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** Accelerated ageing on the actual limiting process in storage (hydrolysis and gelatinisation at controlled T and RH), with enough temperature points to support a rate or lifetime at ordinary storage temperature and humidity, rather than high-T thermo-oxidation. Ink decay is a separate row; it is not filled from the collagen slot, and it stays empty until a measured ink kinetics source exists. A SLOT left empty remains inconclusive, not a kill.
- **Kill condition** (from the persistence lock, once the slot is filled): row 7b counts toward a kill of H-universal only if it is filled and a barrier-held book outlasts its stopped or absent upkeep by ≥ 3 orders of magnitude. H-two-source is KILLED if, at this named level, the book's lifetime does not follow its barrier at all (kill 1: a case that resists classification). An unfilled slot is reported as inconclusive.

## Anhedonia Row B — β alone does it

- **Filed by:** Chemist. Lock `ef8e820`, results `d2585fb`. Scored 2026-11-19 ~19:20 America/Regina in `forge/2026-11-19-1900-anhedonia`. Sister row A ('noisier, not stickier') is scored separately as SURVIVED (weak); see `docs/forge/results-2026-11-19.md`.
- **Hypothesis:** If anhedonia is a β-only (reward-sensitivity) change, anhedonic/MDD choice should be noisier, not stickier — that is, β alone accounts for the pattern. Row B is the premise of that claim (meta-analytic difference in β/ρ and its sign), reported not headline.
- **Why it is unscored:** **NOT-TESTABLE-YET.** The studies opened do not isolate a β-only change. Learning rates also shift: Mukherjee's main effect is a lower learning rate (p = .005/.0007), and Pike's main effect is a higher punishment learning rate. The deficit has more than one dial. Anhedonia-specific data (Ramaswamy et al. 2026) show no group differences in sensitivity or learning rates (BF01 3.4–6.0) and no β effect. The β/reward-sensitivity premise itself is mixed: lower in Huys 2013, Rupprechter 2018, Mukherjee 2020 (punishment) and Pike's conventional meta (SMD −0.215); null in Pike's simulation meta (+0.003) and in Ramaswamy 2026. A clean test of the β-only premise was not available in the opened literature.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** A pre-registered bandit/reversal study with anhedonia measured directly (SHAPS/DARS), a model that includes β, separate reward/punishment learning rates AND a choice-kernel stickiness term, plus parameter-recovery checks for β vs κ trade-off, and model-agnostic overall stay rate reported alongside. (From the scored RESULTS.md missing-experiment note.)
- **Kill condition:** No separate kill threshold is locked for Row B (the lock records it as the premise, reported not headline). Until the experiment above exists, the β-only premise cannot be cleanly tested. What the opened studies already show is recorded in `docs/forge/results-2026-11-19.md` and is not re-scored here.

## Olson institutional sclerosis — interest-group density

- **Filed by:** Historian. Cross-country lock `da40ecb` (forge copy `63930b0`, code `fe4d339`, results `118e0d4`), scored 2026-11-19 in `forge/2026-11-19-1915-olson-score`. US-state item 15 lock `244c20d` (forge copy `651c211`, results `93e28c8`), scored 2026-11-19 ~19:30 CST in `forge/2026-11-19-1930-olson-states`. Both official rows are scored **NOT-TESTABLE-YET**; see `docs/forge/results-2026-11-19.md`. Neither verdict is changed here.
- **Hypothesis:** Olson, *The Rise and Decline of Nations* (Yale UP, 1982): the longer a society has had stable institutions without upheaval, the more narrow interest groups accumulate and lock in rules that serve them, so growth slows. Upheaval clears them out. Predicted sign: b < 0 on ln(1+durable_start) after controlling for initial income.
- **Why it is unscored:** Both locked tests are scored, and both are **NOT-TESTABLE-YET**. Cross-country (Polity5 `durable`): W1 1960–2010 is AGAINST by point estimate, not significant (b = +0.282, p 0.084, N 98); W2 1980–2018 is WEAK (b = −0.026, p 0.853, N 120). `durable` measures regime change, not destruction of interest groups, and is truncated at 1800 or independence. US states, post-1982 (Olson's state age; **rung 1**): W1 1985–2019 is WEAK (b = −0.0076, p 0.936, N 48); W2 2000–2019 is SUPPORT (b = −0.3495, p 0.0116, N 48). Windows disagree, so the combined verdict is not SURVIVED. State age is confounded with region and resources. The 2000–2019 US signal comes from the shale and resource booms in ND, MT and WY, and is NOT counted as partial support.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** A direct measure of entrenched interest-group density, in place of Polity's `durable` or US state age, on data nobody has seen. V-Dem civil-society indices do not qualify. No cross-country long-run series is known. Olson's Encyclopedia of Associations counts cover US states only, and were not the predictor in the item 15 test (that test used state age). The post-1982 US-state age test has now been run and seen. Any re-lock must declare that the 2026-11-19 cross-country and US-state numbers were already seen, and must use an unseen measure or unopened data (for example post-2018 windows).
- **Kill condition** (from the locks, already fixed, for tests that use the locked `durable` or state-age models): KILLED if AGAINST in both windows, or b > 0 with p < 0.05 in either; SURVIVED (= REDUCES-TO-KNOWN) if SUPPORT in both windows; NOT-TESTABLE-YET for any other combination. A later test on a direct density measure would need its own pre-data lock, with the disclosure above.
- **Historian's wording of both tests:** Two locked tests, cross-country and US states, are both NOT-TESTABLE-YET. Proxies for how old a regime or state is can't confirm or kill the hypothesis. A real test needs a direct measure of interest-group density on data nobody has seen. Rung 1.

## Equanimity — three dials (proposed; not yet locked)

- **Filed by:** Proposed in the room (Physicist, with Priest's kill noted below). **CONJECTURE.** There is no committed lock and no scored row. The meditator study described here was not found.
- **Hypothesis (CONJECTURE):** Equanimity is not a single dial. It is three separately measured changes: β (gain, Boltzmann / softmax inverse temperature) lower; loss aversion λ near 1; effort held. Practice must be named, not pooled across meditators.
- **Why it is unscored:** The study design does not yet exist in the published literature as specified, so the row cannot be scored. This is a structural absence. When a lock is filed, Amendment 2 would record it as NOT-TESTABLE-YET with this experiment written out.
- **Experiment that would decide it:** In practitioners of a **named** contemplative practice (not a mixed or pooled "meditators" group), measure the three dials separately on the same people: decision noise / inverse temperature β; loss aversion λ; and effort, with anhedonia screened out and selection checked before any effort drop is interpreted. Compare to a pre-registered control. Do not treat a change in only one dial as support for the three-dial claim.
- **Kill conditions** (as proposed in the room; to be copied into a lock before any run):
  1. Flatness: a lower response to the outcome itself, rather than the three-dial pattern.
  2. Priest's kill: only the learning rate is lower, with β intact.
  3. Lower effort, but only after anhedonia has been screened out and selection has been checked; a drop in effort that is just anhedonia or selection is not support, and is a kill of the "effort held" dial if that is all that moved.

Related, but not this row: Chemist's β-only anhedonia prediction is locked in `forge/2026-11-19-1900-anhedonia/`. Row A ('noisier, not stickier') is scored in `docs/forge/results-2026-11-19.md`. Row B ('β alone does it') and its missing experiment are entered above.
